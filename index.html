Pad settings

The drum module can't tell which hand hit a pad, so the app checks the pad instead. Each part is tied to the pad it's played on. The default is the Songo setup: R on the hi-hat, L on the snare, K on the kick. To change a part, press Learn, hit every pad or zone for it, then press Done.
